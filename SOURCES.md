# Sources — the news of Saturday 21 November 2026

Every story on the poster was reported on, or was unfolding on,
21 November 2026. The poster names the day in its masthead and
keeps each claim short so it stays faithful to the reporting.

## Lead story

- **COP31 talks go into overtime, no deal yet.** The UN climate
  summit in Antalya, Türkiye, slipped past its scheduled Friday
  close with negotiators from nearly 200 countries still divided.
  The revised draft dropped every mention of fossil fuels and a
  proposed $120 billion-a-year adaptation finance target; a
  protest banner reading "failure" was raised in a late-night
  session, Panama warned it would object to any deal that omits
  fossil fuels, and COP31 president Murat Kurum promised a third
  draft on Saturday morning after overnight talks.
  Reported by AP, Reuters, AFP and Carbon Brief watchers.

## Panels

- **Russian missile hits Poland.** A Russian Kh-101 cruise
  missile came down in a field at Nowy Lipowiec in south-eastern
  Poland, about 50 km from the Ukrainian border, with a second
  suspected missile and a jet-powered drone at other sites. No
  one was hurt. Prime Minister Donald Tusk called it an
  intentional provocation timed to the 11-nation Koalicja 2026
  allied exercise; NATO jets were scrambled, and Secretary
  General Mark Rutte was due in Warsaw on Saturday 21 November.
  Reported by BBC News, Reuters, AP and AFP.
- **Anthropic slides as the AI rout deepens.** A day after its
  record Nasdaq debut (opened $111.50, closed +17% at about $97
  on an $83 offer), Anthropic fell about 13% in early trading
  toward its IPO price. On Friday the S&P 500 fell 3.7% and the
  Nasdaq 5.3%; the S&P 500 and the Dow both entered correction
  territory, Nvidia fell more than 11%, and Taiwan's market fell
  about 6% on the day.
  Reported by BBC Business, Reuters and AP.
- **Strike on Kryvyi Rih: 2 dead, 42 injured.** City head
  Oleksandr Vilkul reported two people dead and 42 injured,
  four of them children, in Russia's latest missile and drone
  attacks on Ukraine, as France sought a UN Security Council
  meeting after the week's mass strikes on Kyiv.
  Reported by Ukrainian officials via news wires, 21 November.
- **BepiColombo reaches Mercury today.** The joint ESA–JAXA
  mission, launched on 20 October 2018, begins its capture into
  Mercury orbit on Saturday 21 November 2026 after an eight-year,
  roughly six-billion-mile journey — only the second spacecraft
  ever to orbit Mercury, after NASA's Messenger. Its two
  orbiters, ESA's MPO and JAXA's Mio, separate on 9–10 December.
  Reported by ESA, Sky & Telescope, EarthSky and NPR.
- **Vendor data breach exposes card customer info.** JPMorgan
  Chase, Visa and Mastercard reported cybersecurity incidents
  involving third-party service providers; the banks said their
  core payment networks were not affected, while customer
  information was exposed through the vendor.
  Reported in the day's business and technology coverage.
- **Trump threatens an overseas AI data-centre ban.** President
  Trump threatened to bar US firms from building AI data centres
  overseas, a pledge reported as a new headache for the AI
  industry on a day markets tumbled. Separately, the White House
  removed its cyber-security chief after a series of tech
  outages.
  Reported by BBC News and AP.
